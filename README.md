1. Business Scenario / Background
FashionWave, a mid-sized retail chain with 50 stores across India. The company has been facing stagnant weekend sales,
while weekday sales are consistent. Management wants to understand what drives sales, which products are performing well,
and how discounting affects profitability.
# 2. Business Objective
Increase weekend sales by 15% in the next quarter.
Improve product-level profitability by identifying underperforming items.
Make data-driven recommendations on discount strategies.
# 3. Key KPIs
Total Sales: Total revenue generated per store per week.
Average Order Value (AOV): Revenue per transaction.
Profit Margin: Revenue minus cost.
Units Sold per Product: Number of items sold in each category.
Discount Impact: Difference in profit margin when discounts are applied.
Weekend vs Weekday Sales Ratio

# 4. Business Questions / Challenges
Which product categories generate the highest revenue and profit?
How do weekend sales compare to weekday sales? Identify patterns.
What is the impact of discounting on profit margins?
Which stores are underperforming, and why might that be?
Can you segment customers based on purchase behavior and suggest targeted strategies?

# 5. Constraints / Assumptions
Only data for the last 2 years is available.
Discounts above 30% are limited by policy.
Assume seasonal effects (festivals, holidays) affect sales.

# 7. Deliverables / Expected Output
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Summary statistics
Visualizations showing trends by category, store, and weekend/weekday

# Insights
Hidden trends, patterns, or anomalies in the data
Recommendations
Quick wins vs long-term strategies
Suggested discount strategies
Customer segmentation approach
Dashboard 
Interactive dashboard showing KPIs by store, category, and time period
# Report
8–10 pages summarizing findings, charts, and actionable recommendations use PPT, Pdf, or Word doc to create the report
